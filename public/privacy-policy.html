Privacy Policy

TechnoTrade Academy · Effective: 12 November 2026 · Last updated: 12 November 2026

TechnoTrade Academy ("we", "us") provides trading education only — courses, lessons and learning material. Nothing on this website is investment advice, and no returns are guaranteed. This policy explains what information we collect when you use technoacademy.online, how we use it, and the choices you have.

1. Information we collect

  • Account information: your name and email address when you create an account, and a password (stored only as a secure hash — we never see your password). If you sign in with Google, Google shares your name, email address and profile photo with us.
  • Learning activity: the courses you enrol in, lessons you complete, and your subscription status (monthly or yearly), so we can show you the right content and keep your access working.
  • Payment information: payments are processed by Razorpay. We receive and store the payment ID, amount, and payment status to confirm your subscription. We do not receive or store your card numbers, UPI IDs, or bank details — those go only to Razorpay and your bank.
  • Technical information: basic data your browser sends automatically, such as device type and pages visited, and small amounts of data stored on your device (local storage) to keep you signed in and remember your progress.

2. How we use your information

  • To create and manage your account and sign you in.
  • To give you access to the courses you paid for and track your progress.
  • To confirm payments and handle refunds or payment problems.
  • To contact you about your account, your subscription, or important changes to the service.
  • To keep the service secure and fix problems.

We do not sell your personal information to anyone, and we do not run third-party advertising on this website.

3. Who we share it with

We share limited information only with the services that run this website:

  • Supabase (Lovable Cloud) — hosts our database and handles sign-in.
  • Razorpay — processes your payments.
  • Google — only if you choose "Continue with Google" to sign in.
  • Cloudflare R2 — hosts course videos you watch.

We may also share information if the law requires it, or to protect the security of the service.

4. How long we keep it

We keep your account and learning records while your account is active. If you ask us to delete your account, we delete your personal information from our systems within a reasonable time, except for payment records we must keep for accounting and legal reasons.

5. Your choices and rights

  • You can update your name and account details after signing in.
  • You can ask us for a copy of the personal information we hold about you.
  • You can ask us to correct or delete your information by emailing us (see below). Deleting your account also ends access to your courses.
  • You can sign out at any time; signing out does not delete your account.

6. Children

Our courses are general trading education. If you are under 18, please use this website only with a parent or guardian's permission.

7. Security

We use industry-standard measures to protect your information, including encrypted connections (HTTPS) and access controls on our database. No system is completely secure, so please keep your password private and do not share your account.

Important: Trading involves risk — you can lose money. Our courses teach concepts and real maths for learning purposes only. They are not recommendations to buy or sell any security, and past examples do not guarantee future results.

8. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of the website after a change means you accept the updated policy.
